The finding

The 270M fine-tune only passes when the right answer is silence

Read the top strip again. Every mark in it sits in the ablation and refusal bands on the right — the cases where the correct behaviour is to ask a clarifying question or refuse, and emit no call at all. Across transfers, swaps and multi-turn, it is unbroken empty space.

8.8% is not a score, it's an artefact. FunctionGemma-270M wallet-ft scores 0/101 on transfers, 0/96 on swaps and 0/75 on multi-turn. Its 27 passing cases are all cases where staying quiet is correct.

The untuned base model scores 8.1% the same way. Fine-tuning moved the core task by nothing.

The same dataset and the same LoRA recipe take Gemma-4 E4B from 9.8% to 80.1%. The constraint is model capacity, not training data.
